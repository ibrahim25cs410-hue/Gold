# Data sources

Yahoo Finance GC=F supplies the project's gold-futures price series. The COMEX GC session calendar governs trading hours. Yahoo series prices, individual contract prices, official settlement and XAU/USD spot prices represent different quantities. A provider attribution or code-package license does not grant redistribution rights for market data. Obtain and document authorization for ingestion, retention, evaluation and publication before distributing any derived result that reproduces vendor prices.

- [CME Gold contracts](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/metals/precious/gold.html)
- [CME trading hours](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading-hours.html)
